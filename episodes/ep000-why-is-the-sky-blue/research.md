# Research — ep000

- Rayleigh scattering by air molecules is proportional to 1/λ⁴. Blue (~450 nm) vs red (~700 nm): (700/450)⁴ ≈ 5.9, so "almost six times". Violet (~400 nm): (700/400)⁴ ≈ 9.4.
- Violet scatters more than blue, but sunlight has less violet at the ground, and human eyes are less sensitive to violet; together the sky looks blue.
